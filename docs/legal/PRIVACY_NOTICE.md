# Privacy Notice

LDM processes downloads locally on your device. It does not include telemetry or analytics.

Apart from the downloads you start, LDM makes two requests on its own, both to GitHub: once at start and once a day it asks which version of LDM is the latest, and when you open the settings it asks which version of yt-dlp is the latest. These requests carry LDM's version and nothing about you or your downloads; GitHub sees your IP address, as it does for any visit. You can turn off the LDM check under Settings → Updates. LDM never downloads or installs an update by itself.

LDM may store download URLs, hostnames, filenames, file types, status, progress, history, settings, and cleaned error reports in local app state.

The browser companion can detect links, media candidates, and safe request metadata needed to create a download job. LDM does not automatically read browser cookies, sign-in data, account data, Authorization headers, or Proxy-Authorization headers.

Reports are copied only when you click the report action. Reports omit query values, header values, cookies, credential values, and local file paths.

History and local state stay on your device. You can delete history entries from the app.
